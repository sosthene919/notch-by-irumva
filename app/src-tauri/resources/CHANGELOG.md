# Release notes

## 1.2.6

- Maximized apps now get out of the way too: when any app is maximized on the notch's display, the whole notch retracts and stays hidden until the window is restored or minimized — not only in full screen. (Settings > "Hide when an app is full screen or maximized".)
- The closed pill is about 15% taller — it read as a sliver against the taskbar.
- Every hub page now fits inside the fully expanded notch without its bottom rows being clipped: Pomodoro (ring, timer, presets), Music (art, timeline, controls — and lyrics use the space that's left instead of pushing them out), Water (all three chip groups, in compact rows), plus Today, Shelf, Reminders and Devices. The expanded song view and notification banner grew and compacted the same way.
- Opening the hub feels snappier: a shorter press-and-hold and a quicker, stiffer opening spring.
- The UI now uses the OnlySans typeface whenever a copy is installed on your PC (Segoe UI until then), and Settings > About now links to Instagram (@iwasaki.10) and Ko-fi (ko-fi.com/iwasaki10) if you'd like to follow or support the project.

## 1.2.5

- The notch is 18% larger again in every view — 1.2.0 shrank it too far. Same proportions, just back to a comfortable size.
- Full-screen is now detected across every window on the notch's display, not only the foreground app: the whole notch retracts into the top edge the moment any app goes full screen, and it returns as the compact pill once none is.
- When nothing is playing, the Music pane now shows a vinyl record cover — graphite grooves with the red music label — instead of the plain grey square.
- Notifications arrive more quietly: the banner grows from the top edge in one smooth ease-out pass, with the icon, title and actions settling in sequence. No bounce, no glow.
- Motion was given one more pass in Apple's language: artwork and track changes resolve with a soft focus-in, buttons press a touch deeper, pages and lists settle instead of snapping, and the equalizer breathes more smoothly. Reduced-motion settings still turn all of it off.

## 1.2.0

- Settings is now its own full-size popup window, and the notch is 35% smaller in every view — collapsed pill, expanded player and hub alike.
- Expanded views got the finishing pass: Pomodoro's red ring and play pill, TodoList's green done tasks, Music's timeline bar, and the settings slides now live in the popup window.

## 1.1.5

- Settings now opens in its own full-size window (it used to be squeezed inside the notch). The notch stays on screen, changes apply live, and closing the window just hides it.
- The notch is now a wide, panoramic black bar: Music and Calendar sit side by side in the new layout, with a settings gear and a battery pill in the top bar.
- Volume gets its own HUD with a big percentage and a glowing segmented bar you can drag; the Music page uses the matching smooth slider.
- Music looks cleaner: big album art with the red music badge, plain white play controls and the equalizer bars while a track plays. Everything else (Pomodoro, tasks, shelf, water, devices) follows the same black-and-white style.

## 1.1.0

- The notch now fades away whenever an app goes full screen on its display, and returns when you leave full screen (Settings can turn this off).
- A system-tray icon in the hidden-icons area shows or hides the notch, opens Settings, or quits — still no taskbar button.
- Every page (Pomodoro, TodoList, Music, Today, Settings and more) now shares the same black, rounded iOS-style design with smooth page and button animations.

## 1.0.0

First full release.

- The notch lives at the top of your screen and shows music with synced lyrics, timers, reminders and Bluetooth battery, then gets out of the way.
- A file shelf, focus timer, water reminders and to-dos, all stored only on this PC.
- If a new version fails to start three times, the previous version and your data come back on their own.

## 0.1.0

Early builds. The island, media, timers, notifications and settings.
