1. THE SOFTWARE
The Software is a local Windows 11 utility (a "Dynamic Island" style,
always-on-top pill) that shows live activities from your system and your
apps - such as media playback, timers, battery and volume - in a small
surface at the top of your screen. It runs on your device, has no update
server, and does not check for updates by itself. Some optional features use
third-party services you choose to enable (for example, synced lyrics from
LRCLib); those services are governed by their own terms.
